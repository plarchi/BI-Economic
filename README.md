# PowerBI UK Government ICT Project Spending Performance - MSc Data Engineering

This MSc Data Engineering project analyses UK Government ICT programme performance using Power BI and business intelligence techniques.

The objective was to transform public-sector project data into interactive dashboards supporting executive reporting, spending analysis, supplier intelligence, risk management, and portfolio governance.

**Skills Demonstrated:**  
- Power BI
- Data Modelling
- DAX & KPI Design
- Business Intelligence
- Portfolio Analytics
- Risk Analytics
- Data Visualisation
- Dashboard Development
---

### Executive Portfolio Dashboard

Provides a portfolio-level view of government ICT programmes, including project counts, approved budgets, actual spending, forecast trends, and investment KPIs. Designed for senior stakeholders to monitor overall programme performance and support strategic decision-making.

![Homepage Dashboard](Context/Slide1.JPG)
---

### Supplier Intelligence Dashboard

Analyses supplier distribution, contract activity, capability types, and market share across ICT vendors. Supports procurement planning, supply-chain visibility, and supplier concentration analysis through geographic and contract-based insights.

![Supplier Intelligence Dashboard](Context/Slide2.JPG)
---

### Project Business & Spending Analytics

Evaluates forecast spending, economic benefits, and predicted returns across major ICT programmes. Scatter plot analysis highlights high-performing investments, inefficient spending, and projects requiring further review.

![Project Business & Spending Analytics](Context/Slide3.JPG)

---
### Risk & Governance Analytics

Provides visibility into project governance and gateway review stages. Supports PMO reporting by identifying programme risks, governance bottlenecks, and projects requiring additional oversight.

![Risk & Governance Analytics](Context/Slide4.JPG)

---
### Risk Detail & Mitigation Analytics

Allows detailed investigation of project risks, including severity, impact, ownership, mitigation plans, and status tracking. Supports risk management processes and governance reviews across government programmes.

![Risk Detail & Mitigation Analytics](Context/Slide5.JPG)

---
### Critical Project Tracker

Tracks high-priority projects using RAG status indicators, financial metrics, ownership details, and action logs. Enables proactive monitoring and intervention for projects with significant delivery or governance concerns.

![Critical Project Tracker](Context/Slide6.JPG)

---
### Project Outcome

This project demonstrates how Power BI can transform complex government datasets into actionable business intelligence. By combining financial, supplier, governance, and risk data into a unified reporting platform, the solution supports evidence-based decision-making, portfolio oversight, and performance monitoring across large public-sector programmes.
